My research roots are in AI, NLP, and image processing — papers spanning facial feature extraction, opinion mining, and brain connectivity visualisation. What the publications don't show: 13 years mentoring 6+ PG and 12+ UG research projects, and learning that the hardest part of AI research isn't the math. It's knowing which question is worth asking.

I've rejoined Sarvajanik College of Engineering & Technology (SCET), Surat, as Assistant Professor in the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (Information Technology). Coming back to academia feels like coming home.

Alongside teaching, I remain associated with Padhega India — Bodhi Tree Knowledge Services. Our philosophy is simple: no piracy, quality books only.

My long-term research direction: AI-powered analysis of dreams and nightmares — somewhere between NLP, affective computing, sleep neuroscience, and human behaviour. There's a lot of unmapped territory between algorithmic thinking and human cognition. I'd like to help map it.

Open to: research collaborations, AI & NLP labs, mentoring, sleep and mental-health AI, hackathon communities — and conversations that bridge tech, education, and cognition. Love books? We're already connected in a parallel universe. Say hi.
